# Nasab

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers and family members digitizing Arabic genealogical books. They work through long, OCR-derived lists and need fast, low-error transcription with full Arabic and right-to-left support.

## Product Purpose

Turn printed tribal genealogies into a structured digital family tree. Success means users can paste sons and daughters in batches, preserve their book order, revisit earlier families, and export durable structured data.

## Brand Personality

Calm, archival, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative family-tree templates, playful genealogy graphics, dense spreadsheet-like entry, and interfaces that prioritize visualization over accurate transcription.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the current transcription target unmistakable.
2. Optimize for pasting lists, not entering one person at a time.
3. Preserve source order and original Arabic spelling.
4. Make every saved family easy to revisit and correct.
5. Separate people entry from later relationship enrichment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native right-to-left layout and Arabic labels. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, semantic controls, and do not use color as the only state indicator.
